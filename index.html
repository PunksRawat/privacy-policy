Privacy Policy — Uttarakhand Pariksha Last updated: 7 September 2026 Uttarakhand Pariksha ("the app") is an exam-practice app for Uttarakhand state competitive examinations. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. Short version The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. Everything about your practice stays on your phone. What we do not collect We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. The app asks for no permissions other than internet access, which it uses solely to download practice questions. What is stored on your device The app stores the following locally, in a database on your phone: which questions you have answered and whether each answer was correct how long you spent on each question your daily practice streak and practice history questions you have bookmarked your language preference This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to any server, not backed up to us, and not visible to anyone but you. You can erase all of it at any time from Profile → Reset Progress, or by uninstalling the app. What the app downloads The app downloads exam questions, answer options and explanations from our content server (Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India). These requests carry no information about you. As with any internet request, our hosting provider processes the connection itself, including the originating IP address, in order to deliver a response. Children The app is intended for adult candidates preparing for state competitive examinations. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. Third parties The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs and no social media integrations. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any. Changes If a future version adds accounts, analytics or advertising, this policy will be updated and the Play Store data safety declaration changed before that version is released.

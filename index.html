Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at PregaMom+. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

PregaMom+ is a privacy-first application. All of the personal and health data you enter into the app (including but not limited to baby kicks, hydration logs, weight tracking, and symptoms) is stored locally and exclusively on your device.

We do not operate servers to sync, upload, transmit, or harvest your personal health information. Because the data remains on your physical device, we do not have access to it.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect or transmit your data to our servers, we do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or sharing with third parties. Your data is used exclusively within the app to:

  • Display your pregnancy progress and daily health goals.
  • Generate local charts, insights, and reminders.
  • Trigger local device notifications for your daily trackers.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use anonymous, aggregated data collection services (such as Google Play Services) strictly for basic app functionality, crash reporting, and analytics to help us improve the app's stability. These services do not have access to your personal health logs.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you keep the app installed on your device. You have full control over your data. You can delete individual logs or completely wipe all your data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the app's data in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at rawatgadwali@gmail.com.
